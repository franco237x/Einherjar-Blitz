Fuente: `source/cilantro-parts.png`. Prompt exacto: `prompts/cilantro.txt`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: professional ORIGINAL animated 2D lane-defense game character, production cutout rig kit.
Style: playful hand-painted mobile-game cartoon like the varied plants in Plants vs. Zombies 2: exaggerated asymmetric silhouette, strong colored outlines, clean broad cel shadows, charming expressive face, botanically recognizable. ORIGINAL character, do not copy an existing PvZ character. No 3D render, no realistic surface noise, no generic rounded blob with a sprout.
Output ONE square PNG with REAL transparent alpha. EXACTLY NINE separated cutouts in a strict 3 columns by 3 rows layout. No text, labels, numbers, gridlines, background, floor or cast shadow. Large empty transparent gutters between all nine items, keep each centered within its cell with generous margins. Read in row-major order. Facing screen RIGHT in a slight three-quarter view. Matching painted scale and consistent design throughout. Facial expression variants MUST have the exact same body silhouette, proportions and view; ONLY the eyes and mouth change. Do not bake detached rig parts into body expression variants.
Primary request: invent a mischievous CILANTRO herb character, an airy tall fan silhouette, with recognizable deeply lobed coriander leaves, not a fern, tree or cabbage. Forest-green skinny stalk body with a lime-green rounded split leaf face leaning toward the RIGHT, a tiny puckered blowing mouth and curious uneven ivory eyes. Two airy lobed cilantro sprays form a broad asymmetrical leafy pompadour, one lower left and one higher right. Low pale root tuft and tiny green leaf feet. Emerald, apple-green and cool teal shadows, no magical purple glow. All actions are cheerful, not sinister.
Row 1: (1) complete assembled cilantro character including both leaf sprays and base, calm; (2) ONLY central face and slender curved stalk body, no leaf sprays or feet, normal curious face; (3) exact same central body as 2, closed blinking eyes.
Row 2: (4) exact same central body as 2, cheeks puffed and lips pursed blowing RIGHT, determined humorous face; (5) ONLY left airy lobed cilantro spray with a short stalk at its bottom-right attachment; (6) ONLY right tall lobed cilantro spray with a short stalk at its bottom-left attachment.
Row 3: (7) ONLY low root tuft with two leaf feet, wide but delicate; (8) ONLY a small curved aromatic green puff projectile, a few little coriander leaves suspended in its swirl, facing RIGHT; (9) ONLY a larger lively curved aroma gust, pale apple-green translucent wisps and two flying leaves, clearly separated from the projectile.
Readable at 150 px. Avoid flowers, furniture, clothes, humanoid limbs, giant identical round eyes, extra cutouts.
```
