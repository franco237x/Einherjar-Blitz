# Prompts del arte · Zombis vivos

Modo utilizado: **ImageGen integrado**, con `transparent_background: true`. La referencia de estilo de las generaciones fue el elenco original de plantas del proyecto. Los siguientes textos son los prompts exactos enviados. Las imágenes seleccionadas se guardan en `source/`.

## despistado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production transparent 2D zombie game character cutout animation kit.
Input image 1: STYLE REFERENCE ONLY, our existing varied plant cast. Match its bold colored cartoon contours, broad polished cel-shaded patches, asymmetry and expressive game silhouettes. The subject now is one humanoid cartoon zombie; do NOT put plants or scenery in the result.
Primary request: create DESPISTADO, an original basic zombie in a Plants vs Zombies 2 inspired cartoon strategy game. Funny and awkward, not scary or realistic.
Subject design: An ordinary unarmored shambling zombie. Tall and lanky, disproportionately long bean-shaped gray-olive head, one droopy huge off-white eye and a smaller misaligned eye, tiny black pupils looking left, long crooked chin, tiny squashed nose and just one silly rectangular tooth. Sparse crooked black hair tuft on top. Ripped olive collared shirt with loose dull-blue tie, dusty plum trousers cuffed unevenly above very thin green shins, two mismatched battered black shoes. Narrow shoulders and long loose arms, absurd resigned expression. Clean family-friendly cartoon zombie, completely distinct from the short cone worker and bulky bucket worker. Head asset is bald; hair tuft is a separate gear asset.
Style: cohesive hand-painted 2D cartoon game art, warm dark colored outlines, broad clean cel shadows, simplified tactile fabric and skin. Asymmetric caricature, readable small-game silhouette, comically vacant eyes. No 3D, no anime, no realistic flesh, no blood, no gore, no bones exposed.
Composition: one LARGE SQUARE transparent master containing EXACTLY FIFTEEN separate assets in an invisible grid of FIVE COLUMNS and THREE ROWS. Read each row left to right. ONE asset per cell, all within its cell, at least 15% clear transparent padding on all four sides. Fully transparent horizontal and vertical gutters. No art crossing between cells. No painted checkerboard, floor, ground shadow, captions, numbers, labels, text or watermark.
View: three quarter SIDE view facing LEFT, consistent across EVERY part. Assembled character should match all detached parts. All detached limb segments shown in a relaxed near-vertical orientation, proximal shoulder/hip/elbow/knee joint at TOP, distal hand/foot joint at BOTTOM, with soft rounded overlap ends for articulation. Pieces must join into one complete character. Neither head nor torso contains attached arms or legs.
ROW ONE, FIVE CELLS LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. Complete assembled Despistado, wearing its a small crooked sparse black-and-gray hair tuft, no skull or face, leaning slightly forward in a neutral shambling stance, two separate visible legs and two loose arms.
2. Only the bald HEAD WITH SHORT NECK, neutral vacant eyes and relaxed mouth, no hair, hat, torso or limbs. Three quarter view facing left.
3. Exactly the SAME isolated bald head and short neck, identical contour, tilt, shading, proportions and size; eyes closed for blinking. Keep the mouth unchanged.
4. Exactly the SAME isolated bald head and short neck, identical outer contour, tilt, shading, proportions and size; mouth opens widely INSIDE the SAME head silhouette for a silly biting expression, simple teeth and dark mouth, no blood. Eyes focus left.
5. Only a small crooked sparse black-and-gray hair tuft, no skull or face, isolated, exactly matches the assembled character.
ROW TWO, FIVE CELLS LEFT TO RIGHT:
6. Only the clothing-covered TORSO from shoulder to pelvis, short neck connection, chest, belly and clothing hips, NO head, arms, thighs, shins or shoes.
7. Only the FAR/BACK upper arm, sleeve and short green upper arm, shoulder at top, rounded elbow at bottom, isolated.
8. Only the FAR/BACK forearm WITH HAND, green skin, elbow at top, three short cartoon fingers and thumb at bottom pointing slightly left, isolated. No upper arm.
9. Only the NEAR/FRONT upper arm, clothing sleeve and green upper arm, shoulder at top, rounded elbow at bottom, isolated.
10. Only the NEAR/FRONT forearm WITH HAND, green skin, elbow at top and three cartoon fingers and thumb at bottom pointing slightly left, isolated. No upper arm.
ROW THREE, FIVE CELLS LEFT TO RIGHT:
11. Only the FAR/BACK thigh, trouser-covered upper leg, hip connection at top, rounded knee overlap at bottom. No calf, shoe or torso.
12. Only the FAR/BACK lower leg AND SHOE, skinny or solid calf as appropriate, rounded knee connection at top, shoe flat sole at bottom with toe projecting LEFT. Neutral near-vertical shin. No thigh.
13. Only the NEAR/FRONT thigh, trouser-covered upper leg, hip at top, rounded knee overlap at bottom. No calf, shoe or torso.
14. Only the NEAR/FRONT lower leg AND SHOE, knee at top, flat sole at bottom and toe projecting LEFT, neutral near-vertical shin. No thigh.
15. A small isolated cartoon beige-gray impact dust puff with two simple pale yellow impact ticks. No body parts or blood.
Constraints: genuine alpha transparency, fifteen assets exactly, one coherent character and consistent limb colors, clean rounded joints, left-facing shoes, no duplicated attached accessories on the bald head. The three head expressions MUST share the exact same head outline and neck position. Keep costume and face original rather than copying a named existing character.
```
